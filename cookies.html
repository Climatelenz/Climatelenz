Section 01

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They allow the site to recognise your browser on later visits, remember preferences, and gather basic usage information.

We also use similar technologies — such as local storage and pixel tags — that operate in comparable ways. Throughout this notice, "cookies" refers to all such technologies.

Section 02

Categories we use

We group cookies into two categories:

  • Essential. Strictly necessary to provide the website and remember your language and consent preferences. These do not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive.
  • Analytics. Help us understand how visitors find and use the site, in aggregate, so we can improve it. These require your consent.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Section 03

Cookies in detail

The cookies listed below are representative. The exact set may change as we update the site; we keep this notice in sync.

Cookie Category Purpose Duration
cl_consent Essential Records your consent choices for non-essential cookies so we don't ask again on every page. 12 months
cl_lang Essential Remembers your preferred language (EN, DE, or FR). 12 months
cl_session Essential Maintains state during a single visit — for example, an open form draft. Session

_ga_<id> Analytics Google Analytics; persists session state for a specific property. Set only after you accept analytics cookies. 2 years
Section 04

Third-party cookies

We use Google Analytics (provided by Google) for aggregate website analytics. These cookies are set only after you grant consent through our cookie banner, and consent is managed via Google Consent Mode — analytics storage stays disabled until you opt in. Google may process this data outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards; see Google's own privacy and cookie notices for details. Where any other third party may set cookies — for example, an embedded calendar or video — that party's own cookie notice applies, and we will identify any such embeds clearly on the page where they appear.

Section 05

Managing your preferences

You can manage cookies in two ways:

  1. On the site. Use the cookie banner shown on your first visit, or open the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer at any time, to grant or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies.
  2. In your browser. Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. See the help pages of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge for instructions.
Note. Disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the site — such as language switching or form drafts — from working properly.
Section 06

Updates to this notice
