Is this report actually audit-ready?
Yes. Every datapoint is traced to a source document. Every calculation uses an ESRS-approved methodology. Every narrative follows the standard's required structure. We've passed 40+ audits. If your auditor flags a material error we missed, we fix it free.
How is this so much cheaper than Big 4?
We don't have marble offices, junior analysts learning on your dime, or partners charging €500/hour to review their juniors' work. One expert, a purpose-built EU-aligned workflow, and zero overhead. That's the entire cost structure. That's the whole org chart.
What if I don't have all the data?
We'll tell you exactly what's missing after our free readiness check. For most mid-size companies, 90% of what you need already lives in your annual report and utility bills. We help you find the rest — usually it's two phone calls to facilities and HR.
Do you do Double Materiality Assessment?
Yes, included in our Full Assurance tier — not an add-on. We facilitate a 2-hour workshop, analyse your stakeholder impacts and financial risks, and draft the assessment. That's the process of figuring out what sustainability issues actually matter to your business and your stakeholders. Standalone, it usually runs €15K+.
What about the new Omnibus I simplified standards?
We're already aligned. The December 2025 amendments reduced mandatory datapoints by 61% and changed materiality thresholds for many sub-standards. We only report what's required for your company size and sector. No voluntary disclosures unless you specifically want them.
